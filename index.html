01 — Profile

About

I'm an Aeronautical Engineer (BE, Rajalakshmi College of Engineering, Anna University) currently working as a Research Engineer Associate at IIT Madras' National Centre for Combustion R&D (NCCRD), where I study flame structure, combustion stability, and fuel flexibility for gas turbine and aero-engine applications. My work spans CFD simulation, optical diagnostics (OH-PLIF, PDPA, spray imaging), and data-driven analysis using Python — with a growing focus on hydrogen, ammonia, and methane as low-emission propulsion fuels.
